In this repository (the Licenciatura thesis on UMD/SD azimuthal asymmetries at the Pierre Auger Observatory), the user pushed back hard on a specific physics-analysis mistake and asked Claude to think it through properly rather than patch around it. Claude had found a numeric result in Bertou & Billoir (GAP-2000-017) — that muon density measured at increasing atmospheric depth *grows* rather than falls, over the range that paper tested — and proposed it as a candidate mechanism to help explain the SD's azimuthal-asymmetry sign inversion. In combining it into a quantitative prediction, Claude applied the same correction factor to the UMD side of the comparison too, degrading an otherwise-good UMD match, without ever flagging that Bertou & Billoir's paper and measurement are fundamentally about the SD — a Water Cherenkov Detector tank with roughly 1.2 m (~120 g/cm²) of water as interacting mass, and a muon energy threshold near zero — not the UMD, a thin buried plastic scintillator under ~2.3 m of soil (~540 g/cm² overburden) imposing a ~1 GeV/cosθ muon threshold.

The user's objection: a finding measured in the SD's own detector and threshold context must not be assumed to transfer to the UMD without first checking whether it actually depends on the SD's much larger interacting mass (e.g. secondary-particle production inside the tank's own water volume being miscounted as "muon signal") or on the SD's much lower muon energy threshold (the SD is sensitive to a soft, low-energy muon population that the UMD's soil shielding removes entirely). Treating "muon physics measured by an SD-focused paper" as automatically detector-agnostic is the error — it could just as easily be an artifact specific to the WCD tank's own construction or to a muon population the UMD never sees at all.

This is a durable, repo-specific lesson: whenever a future session in this repo borrows a numeric or physical finding from SD-focused literature (GAP notes, papers, or simulation output) to inform a UMD-side prediction or a combined SD/UMD comparison, it must explicitly check and state whether that finding is plausibly dependent on the SD's interacting mass or its threshold before applying it to the UMD — rather than assuming any muon-physics result from an SD paper carries over unchanged to a detector with a completely different construction and energy sensitivity.
